    Before You Call

    Know the terms

    The VA and U-M will throw these words at you. Walk in already knowing them — that's how you stay in control of the call.

    Chapter 35 / DEA
    Dependents' Educational Assistance — your benefit, because your dad is 100% Permanent & Total.
    P&T
    Permanent & Total — a 100% VA disability rating not expected to improve. It's what makes you eligible.
    COE — Certificate of Eligibility
    The VA's official letter proving you're entitled to Chapter 35. The school can't pay you until they see it.
    SCO — School Certifying Official
    The person at U-M's Veteran Certification office who reports your enrollment to the VA. No certification = no payment.
    Section 702 / §3679(c)
    The federal law that forces U-M to charge you in-state tuition as a Chapter 35 user.
    FAFSA
    Free Application for Federal Student Aid — unlocks Pell grants, federal loans, work-study, and U-M aid. VA money doesn't count against it.
